How the match is calculated
  1. A trained model compares color and tone features from both photos and predicts Lightroom-style settings. Inference runs in this browser; photos are not sent to an AI service.
  2. The model must first be trained locally on labeled examples: source photo, reference photo and the desired edit settings.

If no trained model is installed, the existing calculation-based matcher is used and identified as a fallback. Model training and dataset instructions are in docs/ai-training.md. Local masks, grain and sharpening are not modeled.
